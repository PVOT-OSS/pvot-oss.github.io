Donate

Keep thelights on.

Pvot is free and always will be. If it has made your phone feel more like yours, a small tip helps more than you would think.

{% include contribute-nav.html %}

Every app, build and late-night fix is volunteer time. There are no ads to pay for it, no data to sell, and no feature will ever sit behind a paywall. That is the point.

What donations do cover is the boring, real stuff: build servers, CI minutes, Play Store fees, and test devices, so that more phones get Pvot and more bugs get caught before you see them.

There is zero pressure. Using the apps, reporting bugs and telling a friend all help too.
