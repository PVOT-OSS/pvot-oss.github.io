Pvot Feed

Pvot Feed is a news aggregator. It does not write or edit news: every article comes from, and links back to, the outlet that published it. For questions about the app, the outlets it offers, or an article you read in it, email {{ site.contact_email }}. Requests about an article's content itself are best sent to the outlet named on it as well.
