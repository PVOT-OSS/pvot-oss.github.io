The system

Built like an OS.
Not a data business.

Pvot forks AOSP and LineageOS at the framework level, so the whole stack is yours to read, build, and trust. Privacy is not a setting here. It is the architecture.

lock

Privacy by architecture

No trackers, no ad IDs, no phone-home. Apps request only the permissions they truly need.

Forks of platform_frameworks_base and system_core: a real, buildable, modern Android base.
