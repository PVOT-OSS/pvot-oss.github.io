Privacy

We collect nothing.

The short version: Pvot has no servers taking your data, no analytics, and no trackers. Here is the detail.

The apps

Pvot apps are offline-first. They store your data on your device and request only the permissions they genuinely need to do their job. There are no ad IDs, no analytics SDKs, and no background phone-home. Where an app talks to the network at all (for example, a weather forecast), it is for a feature you asked for, and it is documented in that app’s source.

The system

Pvot OS forks AOSP and LineageOS and removes Google’s proprietary services. Because the platform is open source, you can audit exactly what it does and build it yourself.

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics or set any cookies. In normal operation:

  • No cookies, no local storage tracking, no analytics scripts.
  • GitHub Pages may log basic request data (such as IP addresses) as part of serving the site. That is GitHub’s infrastructure, covered by their privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts are used for typography and icons, which involves a request to Google’s font servers. No account or identifier is attached.
  • Links out to GitHub, Telegram, and app stores are governed by those services’ own policies.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, open an issue on our issue tracker or reach us on Telegram.
