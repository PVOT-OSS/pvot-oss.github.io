About

Android thatanswers to you.

Pvot is an open-source Android ecosystem: a full suite of first-party apps and the OS underneath them, built by a small team that thinks your phone should work for you alone.

What Pvot is

Pvot is a de-Googled Android platform built from the framework up. We fork AOSP and LineageOS and rebuild the pieces that matter, then ship a complete set of apps to replace the defaults. Every app is open source, offline-first, and free of trackers.

One design system

Every Pvot app is built on PvotLib, our shared Jetpack Compose design system. That is why the whole ecosystem moves and feels like a single product rather than a pile of unrelated apps. The same components that ship in the OS are the ones the apps are made from.

Privacy is the architecture

Privacy is not a settings screen you have to find. It is how the system is built:

  • No trackers, no ad IDs, no phone-home. Apps hold no network permission they do not need.
  • Offline-first. Your data lives on your device by default, not on someone else's server.
  • Open end to end. From the framework forks to the apps, every line is public and buildable.

Who builds it

Pvot is built in the open by volunteers who care about low-level Android. It shares its roots and some of its people with the custom-kernel world, and like that work, it runs on curiosity rather than a business model. If you want to help, the contribute page is the place to start.

Pvot is licensed under Apache-2.0 and individual apps under their own open-source licenses.
